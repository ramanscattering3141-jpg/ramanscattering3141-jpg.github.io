
## From ecg-engine (publication audit) — FYI, no interface changes
- No exported type/field renamed or removed. Behaviour changes you may see in the 3-D heart / explain panel: (1) the WPW delta-wave component ('ramp' shape) now fades smoothly; a fused pre-excited beat is never wider than full pre-excitation (hisDelay unchanged); (2) at K⁺ ≥ 8.5 activation components are time-smeared (sine-wave stage) and the T comp is discordant; (3) PE adds a comp tagged 'superiorly displaced initial forces (q in III)' (was 'altered initial forces (q in III)'); (4) Osborn comp direction changed; (5) takotsubo T direction changed; (6) after a PVC the AV node stays refractory longer (pvcBigeminy: every post-PVC sinus P is blocked, sim.atrial[].conducted false). The `'T wave'` tag you look up is unchanged.
